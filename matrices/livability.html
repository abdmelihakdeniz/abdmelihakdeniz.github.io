Why these scores

  • Eskişehir: A compact city that is easy to get around by tram and high-speed train. Its large student population gives it a young, open atmosphere. Its weakness is a narrower range of jobs and amenities.
  • İzmir: The most balanced big city. Low social pressure, strong on the sea and aesthetics. Costs have risen in recent years.
  • Istanbul: Unmatched on amenities, with high anonymity. Cost, traffic and safety pull its score down.
  • Ankara: Strong public-sector, education and healthcare infrastructure, and relatively affordable. Weak on aesthetics and walkability; a car-centric city.
  • Antalya: The best on nature, but tourism has pushed rents very high. The job market is narrow and seasonal.
  • Bursa: Strong industry and reasonable costs, but a more conservative social fabric and heavy traffic.

Method and limits

Scores are qualitative estimates based on general knowledge, not published indices. The ranking is highly sensitive to the weights; for example, giving job opportunities a high weight pushes the industrial cities up.
